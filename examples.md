# Risk Assessment Examples

The following examples demonstrate how a risk scoring matrix can be applied to different types of organizational risk.

These examples are illustrative only. Organizations should define their own scoring criteria and thresholds.

## Cybersecurity Risk

### Unauthorized Access

**Risk:** Unauthorized users gain access to sensitive information.

**Likelihood:** 4 – Likely

**Impact:** 5 – Severe

**Score:**

`4 × 5 = 20`

**Potential controls:**

* Multi-factor authentication
* Access reviews
* Privileged access management
* Security monitoring
* Employee awareness training

**Treatment:** Reduce

---

## Operational Risk

### Critical Supplier Disruption

**Risk:** A critical supplier becomes unavailable and disrupts business operations.

**Likelihood:** 3 – Possible

**Impact:** 4 – Major

`3 × 4 = 12`

* Supplier monitoring

* Inventory buffers

## Comparing Risks

A risk register can bring multiple assessments together:

| Risk                | Likelihood | Impact | Score |
| ------------------- | ---------: | -----: | ----: |
| Unauthorized access |          4 |      5 |    20 |
| Vendor failure      |          3 |      5 |    15 |
| Project delay       |          4 |      3 |    12 |
| Supplier disruption |          3 |      4 |    12 |
| Regulatory risk     |          2 |      5 |    10 |
| Cost increase       |          3 |      3 |     9 |

This makes it easier to identify which risks may require immediate attention.
